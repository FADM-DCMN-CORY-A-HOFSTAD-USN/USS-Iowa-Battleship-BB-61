🚀 F-22 Raptor Rail-Launch & Hydro-Recovery Concept
---------------------------------------------------

Deploying a traditional land-based, air-dominance fighter like the F-22 from a battleship requires bypassing a conventional runway through alternative launch and recovery hardware:

-   Magnetic/Kinetic Rail Launch: The Raptor is mounted to a high-velocity launch rail system running along the aft or midship deck. The ship's main power infrastructure provides the instantaneous high-amp current needed to accelerate the airframe to flight velocity within a compact footprint, eliminating the need for a full-length flight deck.
-   Aft Deployment Basin: To handle recovery without an arresting wire or vertical landing capabilities, the ship utilizes a specialized deployable floating deck or water-skimming capture system dragged behind the vessel.
-   Material Hardening: Operating a stealth aircraft natively in a maritime environment requires a continuous automated freshwater washdown system to prevent saltwater intrusion from degrading the radar-absorbent coatings and avionics bays.
